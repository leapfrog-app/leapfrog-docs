# Organizations

Organizations group workspaces and manage billing. Each organization has its own subscription plan and member access.

## Organization types

- **Personal**: Individual user organization
- **Team**: Multiple members can be invited

## Managing organizations

Access organizations from the organizations page. View all organizations you belong to and switch between them.

### Creating an organization

1. Go to the organizations page
2. Click **Create Organization**
3. Enter organization name and billing email
4. The organization is created and you're set as owner

### Organization settings

View organization details in **Workspace Settings > Organization**:

- Organization name
- Organization type
- Current plan
- Billing email
- Billing address
- Created date

## Billing

Manage billing in **Workspace Settings > Organization > Billing**.

### Plans

- **Free**: 1 workspace, limited features
- **Pro**: Multiple workspaces, full features
- **Custom**: Custom pricing and limits

### Subscription management

**Pro plan features:**

- Multiple workspaces
- Seat-based pricing (€50/month per seat)
- Adjustable seat count

**Managing billing:**

1. Go to **Workspace Settings > Organization > Billing**
2. Click **Manage Billing** to open the Stripe billing portal
3. Update seat count, view billing history, manage subscription

### Seat limits

Each seat allows one active user. If you have 5 seats, 5 users can access the organization's workspaces simultaneously. Increase or decrease seats as needed in the billing portal.

### Subscription status

- **Active**: Subscription is active and billing normally
- **Canceled**: Will remain active until the current period ends
- **Past Due**: Payment failed, requires action

### Upgrading

1. Go to **Workspace Settings > Organization > Billing**
2. Click **Upgrade to Pro**
3. Choose seat count during checkout
4. Complete payment

Only organization owners can upgrade plans or modify billing settings.

## Organization members

Invite members to your organization from **Workspace Settings > Organization > Members**. Organization members can access workspaces within that organization according to workspace permissions.

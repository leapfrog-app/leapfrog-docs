# Organizations and projects

Organizations give research teams a shared boundary for people, projects, indexed research data, usage, and billing. Switch organizations when you need to work with a different team without mixing its research material with another organization's projects.

## Personal and team organizations

Your account starts with a personal organization for your own projects. You can also create or join team organizations and invite members to collaborate.

Use the organization switcher in the dashboard to choose the active organization. The dashboard then shows that organization's projects, transcripts, usage, members, and billing information.

## How projects belong to organizations

Every cloud project belongs to one organization. Its indexed chunks, embeddings, transcripts, and indexing preferences stay associated with that organization.

A local workspace has a random, non-sensitive workspace identity. Leapfrog uses that identity together with the active organization to find the correct cloud project:

```text
active organization + local workspace identity = cloud project
```

The local workspace does not store the cloud project's database ID. This prevents a project owned by one organization from being reused by another.

## Switching organizations

When you open the same local workspace under a different organization, Leapfrog treats it as a separate organization-owned project:

- the existing index is not copied or transferred
- the previous organization keeps its project and research data
- members of the new organization cannot search the previous organization's index
- syncing under the new organization creates or updates only its own project

For example, indexing a study in your personal organization and later switching to **Acme Research** does not move the personal project. Acme receives a separate index when the workspace syncs under Acme.

<Note>
  Check the active organization before syncing a workspace. The organization that is active during sync owns the resulting cloud project and index.
</Note>

## Member access

Organization membership controls access to organization-owned projects. Invite and remove members from **Dashboard > Organization**.

Removing a member revokes their access to the organization's dashboard and projects. It does not delete the organization's projects, because other members may still rely on them.

## Project deletion and retention

Switching organizations does not delete projects. To remove an organization-owned project and its indexed project data, delete it from **Dashboard > Projects** while the correct organization is active.

Deletion affects that organization's project only. A separate project created from the same local workspace in another organization remains independent.

## Organization billing

Billing and usage are scoped to the active organization. Personal plans are managed separately from negotiated organization plans.

Organization owners can open **Dashboard > Billing** to view the current plan and contact Leapfrog about activating or changing an organization plan. Member management is available under **Dashboard > Organization**.

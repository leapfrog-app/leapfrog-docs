Leapfrog workspaces are organizational units for managing research documents, codes, and quotations. Workspaces can be personal or shared, supporting both individual and collaborative workflows.

## Types of Workspaces

- **Personal Workspaces**: Private, accessible only to the creator. All documents, codes, and quotations remain private.
- **Shared Workspaces**: Collaborative, allowing multiple users to access and edit documents, codes, and quotations.

## Workspace Contents

- **Documents**: Used for creating, transcribing, and analyzing text-based data.
- **Canvasses**: Visual spaces for mapping relationships and organizing information.

### Favoriting Items

- Mark documents or canvasses as favorites for quick access.

## Creating and Editing Workspaces

- Use the workspace switcher to create a new workspace.
- Edit workspace settings via the workspace switcher menu.

## Workspace Settings

- **Rename**: Change the workspace name.
- **Delete**: Remove the workspace.
- **Share**: Invite collaborators (for shared workspaces).
- **Enable Integrations**: Connect Leapfrog with other tools or services.

### Document Settings

- Manage document fields and settings within the workspace. See [Document Editor](/basics/document-editor) for more information.

### Integrations

- Enable integrations in workspace settings to streamline research workflows with external tools.

### Sharing

- Share workspaces by inviting collaborators via email. Collaborators receive an invitation to join and can work in real time.

## Prerequisites

- Collect audio or video interviews for transcription before beginning synthesis.

## Workspace Setup

1. Create a workspace to organize and collaborate on research projects. See [Workspaces](/basics/workspaces) for details.
2. Transcribe audio or video recordings to convert them into text for analysis. See [Transcribing Interviews](/basics/transcribing-interviews).

## Analyzing Research Data

- Use Leapfrog's transcription and coding tools to process qualitative data.
- Tag key themes and categorize data using the coding system. See [Quotes and Tags](/basics/quotes-and-tags).

## Visualizing and Synthesizing Data

- Use the Canvas to create visual reports and organize findings. See [Canvas](/basics/canvas).
- Apply AI-led research synthesis to identify patterns and insights in coded data. See [Synthesis](/basics/synthesis).

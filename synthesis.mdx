## <span id="interview">1. Interview</span>

Begin by conducting user interviews or research sessions. These conversations are the foundation of your qualitative data. Record your sessions (audio or video) to ensure you capture every detail for later analysis.

## <span id="transcribing">2. Transcribing</span>

Upload your interview recordings to Leapfrog. The platform's advanced AI models transcribe audio and video into accurate, readable text—supporting multiple languages and offering options for redaction and filler word removal. This transcript becomes the basis for deeper analysis.

## <span id="coding">3. Coding</span>

With your transcript ready, use Leapfrog's coding tools to tag and categorize key themes, concepts, and patterns. Coding helps break down complex conversations into manageable insights, making it easier to spot trends and recurring topics.

## <span id="canvas">4. Canvas</span>

Move your coded data onto the **Canvas**—a visual space for mapping relationships, brainstorming, and clustering information. Here, you can organize quotes, notes, and themes, and use AI-powered clustering to automatically group related data points.

## <span id="chat">5. Chat</span>

Leverage Leapfrog's AI-powered chat to ask questions about your data, generate summaries, or get instant feedback. The chat feature acts as your research assistant, helping you interpret findings and explore new angles in your data.

## <span id="analytics">6. Analytics</span>

Finally, use Leapfrog's analytics tools to visualize your findings. Generate charts, cluster maps, and reports that make it easy to communicate insights with your team and stakeholders. Analytics help you move from raw data to clear, actionable recommendations.

---

By following this workflow, Leapfrog enables you to:

- **Capture** rich qualitative data
- **Transcribe** and prepare it for analysis
- **Code** and organize key themes
- **Visualize** relationships and patterns
- **Interact** with your data using AI chat
- **Analyze** and present your findings

Leapfrog's integrated workflow ensures that every step, from interview to insight, is efficient, collaborative, and powered by AI.

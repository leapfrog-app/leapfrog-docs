---
title: Redaction
description: 'Redact sensitive information during transcription so the transcript text is easier to share and review.'
---

Redaction is a transcript-time option that tells Leapfrog to detect and redact sensitive information in the generated transcript text.

## Where to turn it on

You can enable redaction:

- when you start a transcription
- as a default in **Leapfrog Settings > Transcripts**

## What it is for

Use redaction when transcript text might include:

- personally identifying information
- contact details
- other sensitive passages you do not want surfaced as plain transcript text

## Important detail

Redaction applies to the transcript output. Review the resulting transcript if you need stronger compliance guarantees or stricter downstream handling.

For many teams, the right workflow is:

1. transcribe with redaction on
2. review the transcript
3. index and tag the redacted transcript instead of the raw recording notes
